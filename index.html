    About the plan

    • No weights, no balancing on balls or other unstable equipment, and no lifting other people. Clear space, non-slip floor, water nearby.
    • These are starter home sessions, not the whole day's activity. Play, walking, cycling, sport and active travel all count towards the daily total.
    • Any participation earns 10 XP — a full, adapted or partial session — once per activity per day.
    • There are no weight, calorie or appearance goals, no leaderboards and no streaks to lose. Rest days are shown as rest, never as a miss.
    • Planned times are blank until you set them; tap a time on any card to fill it in.
    • The home sessions follow a 4-week plan: Day A on Monday, Day B on Thursday, Day C on Friday (Saturday can be a catch-up). The programme week moves on automatically each calendar week; tap 1–4 at the top to repeat or change a week.
    • Try each new move yourself first and stay nearby for unfamiliar movements. Check the sofa/table edge (Week 3 incline push-ups) and the step (Week 3 step-ups) are stable. The Week 4 wall handstand-lean is only done with an adult present, feet on the floor.
